## Instrucciones de generación

### Portada oscura

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: landscape GitHub README hero backdrop for Umbral, a native macOS utility catalog with a neutral graphite visual identity. A dark high-end architectural studio rendering, showing a three-dimensional graphite square panel with a rounded upper-right notch and a separate small satin-silver rounded square suspended just above that notch, a missing puzzle piece. Composition concentrated in the right 52%, blank charcoal negative space on the left for typography, generous margin above and below. Wide 3:2, frontal with subtle perspective and scale, clean warm-neutral blacks and whites, soft edge lighting, tactile brushed aluminum, quiet contrast. No words, letters, visible UI, laptop, Apple logos, other logos, orange, purple or green. The two pieces form the only focal object.

### Ilustración de la publicación

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset: standalone landscape release illustration for Umbral 1.0, a native Mac utility catalog, neutral identity. Close-up of two precisely machined rounded geometric parts on a soft light-gray studio surface, the larger matte graphite rounded plate featuring a curved recess in one corner and the smaller square in silver floating next to the recess. Abstract product photography, editorial and spare, finely beveled edges, soft shadow, tonal light-grays and graphite, visually distinct from a dark hero banner. 3:2 landscape, object centered with breathing room. No lettering, numbers, Apple marks, computers, UI, watermarks or color accents.
